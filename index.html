Our Process

From insight
to impact.

A structured, research-backed methodology that transforms observations into measurable UX improvements.

01
Discover & Listen
We conduct user interviews, session analysis, and behavioral data review to understand where and why users fail.
02
Diagnose & Model
Map cognitive bottlenecks against psychological frameworks — Fogg, AIDA, dual-process theory and more.
03
Design & Prototype
Create evidence-based solutions — from micro-copy to full interface redesigns — and validate with rapid testing.
04
Measure & Iterate
Track KPIs, run A/B tests, and continuously refine until the numbers confirm the psychology.
